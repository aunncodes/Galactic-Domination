# I Was Reincarnated as a Warlord Duck in Space and Now I’m Trying to Grow My Empire
> [!NOTE]
> Also known as Galactic Domination

A strategy game similar to *Sort the Court!* about conquering space!

Made with React and Vite, includes music, a custom introduction sequence, and 15+ unique characters to interact with!

- Manage your resources carefully
- Conquer neighboring planets
- Become the ruler of space!
